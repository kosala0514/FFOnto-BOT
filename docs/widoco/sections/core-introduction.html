Introduction back to ToC

Forest-fire situational awareness depends on several kinds of evidence at once: camera and drone images, audio clips from the field, and weather / Fire Weather Index (FWI) records. Machine-learning models turn this evidence into labels, captions, scene descriptions and scores. Such outputs are uncertain, often disagree between modalities, and are hard to query together.

FFOnto (Forest Fire Ontology) is the schema of a knowledge graph that integrates these modalities and answers questions about them. Version 2 was rebuilt from scratch from competency questions (CQs): the {{N_CQ}} CQs listed below were derived from the questions of the ForestFire-VQA dataset, and every term in the ontology is used by at least one CQ query.

The ontology is published as three modules:

ModuleNamespaceContent
Core schema (this document)ffo: http://purl.org/ffonto# {{N_CLASSES}} classes, {{N_OBJ}} object properties, {{N_DATA}} datatype properties
Controlled vocabularyffv: http://purl.org/ffonto/vocab# {{N_CONCEPTS}} SKOS concepts in {{N_SCHEMES}} concept schemes (entity kinds, states, PPE, hazards, actions, …)
Danger rulesffr: http://purl.org/ffonto/data/rule/ Weather / FWI threshold rules stored as data

Additional resources: the SHACL shapes (data-quality checks), the JSON-LD context (maps the raw image / audio JSON and the weather CSV fields to ontology terms), and the full module files (core, vocabulary, rules).
